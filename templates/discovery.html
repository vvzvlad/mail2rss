mail2rss — link calculator

Everything on this page is computed in your browser (WebCrypto): the secret and the mailbox id are never sent anywhere. Get mailbox ids from the CLI with mail2rss folders; an OPML with all folders comes from mail2rss opml.

The calculator cannot run: WebCrypto is unavailable or its self-test failed. WebCrypto requires a secure context — open this page over HTTPS (or on localhost).
